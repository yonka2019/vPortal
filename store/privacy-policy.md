# VPortal — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-30_

VPortal is a new-tab extension. It collects **no user data**.

## What the extension does

- Your links, sections, settings and click counts are stored **only in your browser**
  (`chrome.storage.local`), on your device.
- Nothing is transmitted anywhere. The extension makes **no network requests** and has
  no host permissions. All fonts, icons and code ship inside the extension package.

## What we don't do

- No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.
- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No cookies.
- No data shared or sold to third parties — there is nothing to share.

## Your data, your control

- Export/Import lets you back up your links as a local file you keep.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data.
